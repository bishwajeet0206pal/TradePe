3. Open Account

Ship fabrics first, buyer transfers payment 60 days after receipt.

Exporter Risk:Very High
Buyer Risk:Very Low
Verdict:Exporter rejected
Why use a Letter of Credit (LC)? In export trade, since buyer and exporter reside in different countries with no prior relationship, banks act as neutral third-party trustees. The buyer's bank guarantees payment to the exporter, protecting you against buyer defaults, while protecting the buyer from paying until physical dispatch proof (Bill of Lading) is audited.
